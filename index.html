07 · faq

%man paid

is this financial advice?

No. PAID shows what devs paid for on DexScreener. It never says buy, and it never will.

does dex paid mean a coin is safe?

No. It means the dev spent their own money on the coin's page. That's a signal, not a promise. A dev can pay and still sell.

where does the feed come from?

DexScreener's public API: latest profiles, boosts, community takeovers and ads, plus the paid-orders endpoint for each coin. No key. Your browser reads it directly every few seconds.

is the desk buying for real?

Not yet. The desk is in beta: it runs your rules on the real feed and shows what it would buy. Nothing executes, no wallet connects and no funds move.

what can't it do?

It can't see a payment before DexScreener publishes it, and a profile only shows up once it's approved. Launch→paid uses the earliest pair DexScreener has for the coin.

will paid DM me?

Never. Anyone who does is not us.
